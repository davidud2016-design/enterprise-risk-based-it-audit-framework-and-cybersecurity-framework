# ISO27001 – NIST CSF – SOX ITGC Mapping

## Purpose
To reduce duplication and align compliance efforts across frameworks.

---

## Example Mapping

| Control Area | ISO27001 | NIST CSF | SOX ITGC |
|-------------|----------|----------|----------|
| Access Control | A.9 | PR.AC | Logical Access |
| Change Management | A.12 | PR.IP | Change Controls |
| Logging & Monitoring | A.12.4 | DE.CM | Monitoring Controls |

---

## Benefits
- Streamlined audits
- Reduced audit fatigue
- Stronger, unified assurance
