# Control Strength Scoring Model

## Objective
To evaluate IT and cybersecurity controls based on **effectiveness**, not just existence.

---

## Scoring Dimensions

### 1. Design Effectiveness
- Control addresses the identified risk
- Control aligns with relevant standards

### 2. Operational Effectiveness
- Control operates consistently
- Evidence supports sustained execution

### 3. Governance & Ownership
- Clear control ownership
- Monitoring and escalation mechanisms exist

---

## Scoring Scale
- **1 – Weak:** Control ineffective or absent
- **2 – Developing:** Control exists but inconsistently applied
- **3 – Effective:** Control operating as designed
- **4 – Optimised:** Control embedded, monitored, and continuously improved

---

## Usage
Scores support remediation prioritisation and maturity tracking.
