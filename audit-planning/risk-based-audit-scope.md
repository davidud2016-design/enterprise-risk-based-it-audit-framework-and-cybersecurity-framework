# Risk-Based Audit Scope Definition

## Objective
To define an audit scope that prioritises systems and processes based on **business impact and risk exposure**.

---

## Risk Factors Considered
- Financial reporting impact
- Regulatory exposure
- Data sensitivity
- System criticality
- Threat likelihood
- Historical audit findings

---

## Scoping Methodology
1. Identify critical business processes
2. Map supporting IT systems
3. Assess inherent and residual risk
4. Prioritise systems for audit coverage
5. Align scope with applicable standards

---

## Output
A focused audit plan that concentrates effort on high-impact, high-risk areas while maintaining regulatory coverage.
