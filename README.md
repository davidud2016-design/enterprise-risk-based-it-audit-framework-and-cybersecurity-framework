 # Enterprise Risk-Based IT Audit & Cybersecurity Framework

## Overview
This repository provides a **risk-based IT audit and cybersecurity governance framework** designed to help organisations move beyond checklist-driven compliance toward **measurable cyber resilience**.

The framework integrates globally recognised standards including:
- ISO/IEC 27001
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- SOX IT General Controls
- Cybersecurity maturity model principles

It is intended for IT auditors, cybersecurity professionals, risk managers, compliance teams, and technology leaders operating in regulated or complex digital environments.

---

## Why This Framework Exists

Many organisations successfully pass audits yet continue to experience:
- Control breakdowns
- Cybersecurity incidents
- Recurring audit findings
- Poor alignment between business risk and IT controls

This framework addresses those challenges by:
- Prioritising **risk impact over checklist compliance**
- Measuring **control strength**, not just control existence
- Integrating audit, cybersecurity, and governance functions
- Supporting continuous improvement and audit readiness

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Risk-Driven Audit Planning
Audit scope is determined by business impact, threat exposure, and regulatory risk rather than uniform control testing.

### 2. Control Strength Evaluation
Controls are assessed based on design effectiveness, operational effectiveness, and governance maturity.

### 3. Standards Integration
ISO27001, NIST CSF, and SOX requirements are mapped to reduce duplication and strengthen assurance.

### 4. Continuous Improvement
Audit outcomes are used as inputs for control enhancement and cybersecurity maturity growth.
