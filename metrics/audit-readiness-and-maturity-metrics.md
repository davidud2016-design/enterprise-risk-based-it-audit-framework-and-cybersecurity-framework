# Audit Readiness & Control Maturity Metrics

## Purpose
This document defines key metrics used to measure **IT control effectiveness, audit readiness, and cybersecurity maturity** across enterprise environments.

These metrics support data-driven decision-making by audit, cybersecurity, risk, and senior leadership teams.

---

## Key Metrics

### 1. Percentage of Effective Controls
Measures the proportion of assessed controls operating effectively.

**Formula:**  
(Number of controls rated “Effective” or above ÷ Total controls assessed) × 100

**Insight Provided:**  
Indicates overall strength of the control environment and readiness for audit.

---

### 2. Repeat Finding Rate
Tracks how often the same or similar audit findings reoccur.

**Formula:**  
(Number of repeat findings ÷ Total findings) × 100

**Insight Provided:**  
Highlights weaknesses in remediation quality and governance effectiveness.

---

### 3. Average Remediation Cycle Time
Measures the average time taken to remediate audit findings.

**Formula:**  
Total remediation days ÷ Number of closed findings

**Insight Provided:**  
Reflects responsiveness, ownership, and operational maturity.

---

### 4. Control Maturity Distribution
Shows how controls are distributed across maturity levels.

**Example Levels:**  
- Weak  
- Developing  
- Effective  
- Optimised  

**Insight Provided:**  
Supports prioritisation of improvement efforts and long-term maturity planning.

---

### 5. Audit Issue Closure Rate
Measures the percentage of audit issues closed within agreed timelines.

**Formula:**  
(Closed issues within SLA ÷ Total issues) × 100

**Insight Provided:**  
Demonstrates accountability and effectiveness of remediation governance.

---

## Usage

These metrics should be:
- Reviewed regularly by leadership and governance forums
- Used to prioritise remediation and investment decisions
- Tracked over time to measure improvement in audit readiness and cyber resilience

When applied consistently, these metrics help transform IT audits from periodic compliance activities into continuous assurance mechanisms.
