# Cross-Functional Governance Model

## Objective
To ensure consistent ownership and oversight of IT and cybersecurity controls.

---

## Key Stakeholders
- IT Audit
- Cybersecurity
- Legal & Compliance
- Technology Operations
- Business Owners

---

## Governance Checkpoints
- Risk acceptance approval
- Control design validation
- Remediation tracking
- Policy alignment reviews

---

## Outcome
Improved accountability, faster remediation, and stronger control sustainability.
